## 2. The 2-Step Carrier Forwarding (*61*) Setup Cheat Sheet

Contractors **never need to change their existing phone number**. All they do is enter standard carrier conditional call forwarding:

### Step 1: Forward Unanswered Calls
Open your mobile phone keypad, dial the forwarding code for your carrier, and tap **Call**:

| Carrier | Dial Code to Activate Unanswered Forwarding | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **EE / O2 / Vodafone / Three (UK)** | `*61*+442079460192*11*20#` | Forwards after 20 seconds of ringing |
| **Verizon / AT&T / T-Mobile (US)** | `*71+12025550192` or `*61*+12025550192#` | Conditional forwarding on busy/no answer |
| **Telstra / Optus (Australia)** | `*61*+61291234567**20#` | Forwards after 20 seconds |

### Step 2: Test Call in 30 Seconds
1. Have a friend or secondary phone ring your business mobile.
2. Let it ring for 15 seconds without answering.
3. Within 3.2 seconds, the caller receives the automated WhatsApp triage message from your verified business profile.
4. Your personal phone receives an instant WhatsApp ping with their emergency details and a 1-tap call button.

### How to Deactivate Anytime:
Dial `##61#` (UK/Global) or `*73` (US) and press Call. Conditional forwarding is instantly removed.
